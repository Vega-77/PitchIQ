Who is the marked player? Each clip follows one tracked player: a close-up with a yellow arrow over his head, and a wider view with him boxed. A hollow arrow or thin box means the tracker lost sight of him for a moment and is guessing.

Re-asked clips show his whole stretch at every frame. The close-up is there twice: plain on the left, AI-cleaned on the right (the grain taken off). The AI can invent detail, so if the two disagree on a number, trust the plain one. The wider view runs along the bottom.

Type his shirt number (or GK, or any short name if you know him by sight). Slow it down and step frame by frame to catch the number when he turns. If you recognise him from someone you already named, tap him in the list instead. The video time is shown, so you can check the original video too.

Names can change later: ✎ on a name renames that person in every clip, in both halves. Renaming to a name that already exists merges the two into one person.
