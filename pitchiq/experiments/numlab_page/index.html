How to answer

Each grid is one player you named, with his number on top.

Tap every frame where you can read the whole number on his shirt (both digits of a two-digit number).

Leave the rest: back turned away, blurred, half the number, hidden. Those teach the reader “no number here”.

Shift-tap (or right-click) a frame that shows a different player or a different number.
