Pin the pitch

The software needs to know which part of the picture is which part of the field before it can measure anything in metres. You show it by pinning landmarks it already knows: corners, the halfway line, the penalty boxes. Nothing leaves this computer; the pins are saved to a file.

What are you pinning?
Spread the pins out. Four pins bunched in one corner can look perfect and still be wrong everywhere else. Aim for six or more, over both halves, and skip any you can't see clearly.
Click the paint, not the player near it. Zoom in (mouse wheel, pinch, or the + button) and put the pin where the two painted lines cross.
