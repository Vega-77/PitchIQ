The diagnosis below is kept for the record.

The old EB fits:
- **eb01** (frames 90000-91800): the pitch is drawn too big, with the far line on the running track.
- **eb02** (180000-181800, H2): the far touchline sits mid-pitch. Feet on the pitch: 0.15.

Why:
- **The far touchline is not painted.** The grass meets the fence. The top end of the halfway line, at about y ≈ 460 full-res on frame 90000, marks where it is.
- **The gold paint mask sees little at dusk.** The logo filter (`logo=41`) removes most of the centre-circle arc. A smaller margin lets the logo's own stripes in. See `PitchIQ-data/autocal/eb01/paint_mask_variants.jpg`.
- **Stands, sky and fence blobs above the playing surface count as paint.** In that image they are red, and they feed the far-line fit. Frame 91800 has no pitch paint at all.

Gating the gold paint to the playing surface removed the off-pitch blobs.
- With the logo margin at 41 (eb04), the fit is right.
- With the margin at 15 (eb05), feet on the pitch drops to 0.89 and the pitch narrows to 63.2 m. The margin stays at 41.

The checks do not catch a pitch drawn too big (`feet_on_pitch` gave eb01 1.00). Always look at `cal_overlay` by eye.
