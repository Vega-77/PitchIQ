Match stats your coach can actually use.

Tag a match from the sideline on a tablet, and PitchIQ turns it into per-player minutes, goals, cards and team totals — then gives every player their own report. Camera-based tracking is coming; the tagging and reporting work today.

Try the xG sandbox

Coaches need an invite to create a team. Players sign in with the address their coach put on the roster.

How it works

1

Tag the match

An assistant or team manager taps events and substitutions on a tablet as they happen. Works offline — taps sync when the signal comes back.

2

Review the numbers

The coach sees team totals, the full event timeline, and minutes played per player, adjusted for substitutions and re-entry.

3

Publish reports

Finalising a match gives each player their own private report. Players see only their own numbers, never a teammate's.

What's next

The tagging and reporting above are live. Computer-vision tracking — detecting players and the ball from match footage to derive distance covered, possession and expected goals automatically — is in development. Detection has been validated on real match footage; the tracking and calibration layers come next.
