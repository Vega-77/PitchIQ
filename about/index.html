PitchIQ beta

Privacy, contact and credits

PitchIQ is a beta, built for one high school soccer team's season. This page says who can see a student's data, how long it is kept, how to have it deleted and who to ask.

Who can see a student's data

  • The team's coaches see the squad (names, shirt numbers and the address each player signs in with), every match, and every player's report. That includes pictures of players cut from the match video, used to put a name to each figure the camera tracked.
  • Staff a coach invites to run the tagging tablet record matches and see each match's line-up and statistics. They cannot see students' email addresses or the players' reports.
  • A player sees only their own reports, and only once the coach has published them. Players never see each other's reports or the live match data.
  • Nobody else. Every page needs a Google sign-in, and teams cannot be searched or listed. The database enforces this itself (its rules are firestore.rules in the source), not only the pages.
  • The person who runs PitchIQ administers the database, so, like the operator of any hosted service, can reach everything stored in it.

What is stored

  • The name and email address of the Google account you sign in with.
  • The squad: players' names, shirt numbers and sign-in addresses.
  • Each match: what was tagged on the touchline (kick-off, goals, cards, substitutions) and the statistics worked out from the match video, with the player pictures described above.
  • A link to the match video, if the coach adds one. The video itself is not uploaded to this site.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Everything about a team is kept until the team is deleted. To have a player's data removed, ask the coach first: a coach can take a player off the squad, delete their reports, and delete the pictures cut from a match's video. If that does not work, or to have a whole team deleted, email the address under Contact.

Other services the site uses

  • Google Firebase provides the sign-in and the database. Its code loads from Google's servers (www.gstatic.com), so Google sees your IP address, as with any Google sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the pages and keeps the usual server logs.
  • No analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The fonts are served from this site, not from a font service.
