Mark what the ball is doing each time it changes. Stop on the frame where it changes and press the key for what it does from that frame on:

  • G On the ground: rolling, dribbled, at rest. A bounce is G on the touch frame, then A on the next.
  • A In the air: kicked up, bounced up, thrown in, or held in a keeper's hands.
  • T Touched in the air: a header, chest or deflection. The ball stays up, and a new flight starts on this frame.
  • V Not visible: off screen or hidden. Don't guess what happened while it was out of sight; when it comes back, mark what it is doing then.

Each clip starts where the previous one left off, so check the first frame. Press Clip done when finished, even if nothing changed. The green ring is the tracker's pick; the close-up follows it.
